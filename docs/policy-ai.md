## AI Use Policy

**Last updated:** January 20, 2026

### Purpose

This policy explains principles, scope, roles, and user rights related to AI systems used by our products and services.

### Scope

Applies to all AI models, datasets, integrations, and features developed, procured, or maintained by the organization.

## Principles

- **Safety and reliability:** We evaluate models for accuracy, robustness, and failure modes before deployment.
- **Fairness and non-discrimination:** We test for biased outcomes and take steps to mitigate disparate impacts.
- **Transparency:** We provide clear user-facing explanations of AI capabilities and limitations.
- **Accountability:** Owners and reviewers are assigned for each AI feature and decision process.
- **Privacy:** Data used for model training and inference is handled according to our privacy controls.

## Governance & Roles

- **AI Risk Committee:** Reviews high-risk models and approves exceptions.
- **Model Owner:** Responsible for lifecycle, testing, monitoring, and documentation.
- **Privacy & Legal:** Assess data usage, regulatory compliance, and contractual obligations.

## Development & Deployment

1. **Requirement scoping:** Define desired behaviors and success metrics.
2. **Data preparation:** Document sources, consent, and quality checks.
3. **Testing:** Unit, integration, bias, adversarial, and safety tests.
4. **Staged rollout:** Canary or phased deployment with monitoring.
5. **Post-deploy monitoring:** Track performance, drift, and incidents.

## User Rights & Controls

- **Explainability:** Users receive plain-language information about automated decisions affecting them.
- **Opt-out:** Where feasible, users can opt out of automated profiling or personalized decisions.
- **Correction:** Users can request correction of incorrect data used by models.

## Incident Response

We maintain an incident playbook for model failures, harm reports, or data breaches, including rollback and communication plans.

## Documentation

Each model must have a Model Card and Data Sheet with purpose, training data summary, evaluation metrics, limitations, and contact points.

## Contact

For questions or to report issues, contact the AI Governance team at [support@avnlearn.com](mailto:support@avnlearn.com).
